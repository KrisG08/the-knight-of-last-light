# Burial chamber ambience — 2026-11-05

Godot 4.7.2. Five original PCM files generated by tools/make_burial_audio.py:
12-second filtered underground air bed, three 0.9-second resonant drips and a
three-second periodic seal tone. No external samples or license additions.

Region-owned audio node fades the air bed in over 1.5 seconds, places individual
drips spatially at intervals of 3.1–7.5 seconds and emits resonance at the seal.
Seal resonance stops directly on the seal prop's visibility-change signal. All layers route
through SFX and inherit gameplay pause; region removal stops players/timer.
No per-frame audio synthesis; files preload, playback is capped at three voices.
Combat cue system, save schema and progression remain unchanged.

(520 assertions PASS) and .tools/burial-audio-build-final.log. File sample analysis verifies nonzero RMS and peaks

seal-break silence and region removal. An initial test held a reference across
respawn/reload; corrected to observe the current room instance before seal break.

visibility-change connection so seal silence also applies while gameplay is paused.
