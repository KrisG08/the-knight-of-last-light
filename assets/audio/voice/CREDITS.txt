LOCALIZED NARRATIVE SPEECH

Original dialogue and narrative text authored for The Knight of Last Light.
Synthetic speech generated on 2026-11-21, following explicit owner consent,
using Microsoft Edge's online speech service through edge-tts 7.3.1.
Development client: https://github.com/rany2/edge-tts

English: RyanNeural / SoniaNeural (en-GB).
Bulgarian: BorislavNeural / KalinaNeural (bg-BG).
Russian: SvetlanaNeural (ru-RU), used as a narrative reader for all speakers.
French: HenriNeural / DeniseNeural (fr-FR).
Arabic: ShakirNeural / SalmaNeural (ar-EG).

These are synthesized reading voices, not actor recordings, voice clones or
facial lip synchronization. No microphone, voice API, service token, Python
client or runtime synthesis request is included in the game. All clips play
offline. Account information and player saves were not sent for synthesis.

Per-clip source text, localized transcript, voice ID and SHA-256 are recorded in
manifest.json. Output is mono, 24 kHz Ogg Vorbis, with normalized dialogue level.
Local development preparation does not assert commercial distribution clearance
for the synthesis service. No external publication accompanies this build.

The former opening and all existing NPC/victory/ending speech remain preserved.
